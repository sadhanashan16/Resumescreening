ResumeIQ supports, not replaces, human judgement: scores are decision aids and every shortlist should be reviewed by a person. Names and contact details are removed before scoring. Resume files are processed in memory and never stored; the extracted details and scores are saved to your account so you can manage shortlists, and you can delete them at any time.
