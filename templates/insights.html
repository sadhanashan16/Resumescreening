How the match score is built

40% · Text similarity

Cosine similarity between the TF-IDF vectors of the resume and the job description (skills are canonicalised, so “sklearn” = “scikit-learn”).

30% · Skill coverage

Share of the job's required skills found in the resume. Related skills (e.g. MySQL for PostgreSQL) earn half credit.

15% · Role fit

Agreement between the role predicted for the resume and for the job description (Naive Bayes + SVM), with partial credit for neighbouring roles.

10% · Experience

Estimated years (from date ranges and stated experience) versus the years the job asks for.

5% · Education

Highest degree detected versus the level the job requires.

Auto re-weighting

If the job description states no experience or education requirement, that component is dropped and the other weights are re-normalised.
