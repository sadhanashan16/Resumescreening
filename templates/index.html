How it works

Upload

PDF, DOC, DOCX or TXT resumes. Text is extracted in memory.

Extract (NLP)

Skills, education, experience and contact details are pulled from the text.

Vectorise

Resumes and the job description become TF-IDF vectors (unigrams + bigrams).

Classify & rank

Naive Bayes and SVM predict the role; cosine similarity and skill coverage rank fit.

Shortlist

Top candidates with reasons: matched, related and missing skills. Export to CSV.
