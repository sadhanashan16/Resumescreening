ResumeIQ: AI-Based Resume Screening & Job Matching System

ABSTRACT
{{ICO_DOC}}

ResumeIQ automates resume screening and job matching. It extracts skills, education and experience from PDF, DOC, DOCX and TXT resumes using NLP, converts text to TF-IDF features, and applies Naive Bayes, SVM and cosine similarity to rank resumes against a job description, delivering an explainable shortlist and role recommendations through a Flask web app.

PROBLEM STATEMENT
{{ICO_FLOW}}
  • Recruiters get hundreds of resumes per role; manual screening is slow and inconsistent.
  • Human error and bias can overlook qualified candidates and delay hiring.
  • Resumes come in varied formats and layouts, so unstructured text is hard to compare.
  • Candidates rarely know which roles fit their skills.
OBJECTIVES
{{ICO_TARGET}}
  1. Accept resumes in PDF, DOC (plus DOCX, TXT)
  2. Extract skills, education, experience with NLP
  3. Match resumes to job descriptions
  4. Rank and classify with TF-IDF, Naive Bayes, SVM
  5. Generate a top-N shortlist automatically
DATASET
{{ICO_DB}}
{{N_SAMPLES}}synthetic resumes
{{N_ROLES}}job roles · 200 each
{{N_FEAT}}TF-IDF features
  • {{N_TRAIN}} train / {{N_TEST}} test (stratified 80/20) + 5-fold CV
  • Role-catalog generator with noise: hybrid and sparse resumes
CONCLUSION
{{ICO_CHECK}}

ResumeIQ is a working ML web application that turns unstructured resumes into ranked, explainable shortlists and role recommendations. It screened 14 sample resumes in about {{LAT}} s and reached 99.8% hold-out accuracy on synthetic data; validation on real resumes is the key step before production use.

DISCUSSION
{{ICO_BULB}}
  • NB, SVM and the ensemble perform alike (≈0.998); the single error labels a Cybersecurity resume as DevOps, a neighbouring role.
  • Data is synthetic, so scores are not a real-world accuracy estimate.
  • All 5 hand-written resumes, unseen by the generator, matched the right role. Limits: rule-based extraction, no OCR.
